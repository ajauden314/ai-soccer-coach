Under The Hood

4-Stage CV & ML Pipeline

Combining pose estimation, dimensionality reduction, spatiotemporal action classification, and continuous biomechanical regression.

STAGE 01

Pose Estimation & Keypoints

Uses MediaPipe and YOLOv8-Pose to extract 2D/3D skeletal joint coordinates (ankles, knees, hips, shoulders) directly from practice video.

Input: 30–60 FPS Smartphone Video
STAGE 02

Dimensionality Reduction

Applies PCA & keypoint feature compression to distill dense frame-by-frame coordinate sequences into low-latency biomechanical vectors.

Output: Normalized Joint Trajectories
STAGE 03

Action Classification

3D CNNs & Video Transformers trained on SoccerNet, Kinetics-400, and custom solo drills classify the skill move and form quality.

Labels: Proper / Needs Fix / Injury Risk
STAGE 04

Biomechanical Regression

Predicts continuous metrics including limb angular velocity, joint-angle accuracy, execution success probability, and ACL/ankle strain risk.

Output: Telemetry + 3-Word Audio Cue
Responsible Sports AI

Ethics, Safety & Engineering Focus

Built with youth athlete privacy, anatomical fairness, and real-world pitch constraints at the center of our engineering roadmap.

Athlete Video Privacy

Skeletal keypoints are extracted locally on-device whenever possible so youth players retain full control over their raw training footage without unnecessary cloud video retention.

Diverse Body-Type Calibration

Joint coordinate vectors are normalized by limb-segment ratios rather than raw pixel distances, ensuring fair biomechanics evaluation across varying heights, ages, and builds.

Biomechanical Safety Guardrails

Prioritizes injury prevention over raw ball power—flagging inward knee collapse (valgus), locked-knee landings, and over-rotation before recommending higher-intensity reps.

Real-World Engineering Challenges

Actively solving outdoor pitch glare and tripod angle variance, mobile thermal/latency budgets, and hands-free audio cues that trigger cleanly between reps.

AI Soccer Coach — Project Blueprint

Launchpad Project Team

Core Problem Statement

Ambitious youth soccer players training solo often build bad habits, waste practice time, and risk injury because they lack immediate, objective feedback on their skill execution.

AI & Computer Vision Pipeline

STAGE 01 • POSE ESTIMATION

MediaPipe / YOLOv8-Pose

Extracts 2D/3D skeletal joint coordinates from standard smartphone practice videos.

STAGE 02 • DIMENSIONALITY REDUCTION

PCA / Feature Compression

Streamlines dense frame-by-frame joint trajectories for low-latency mobile inference.

STAGE 03 • CLASSIFICATION

3D CNNs / Video Transformers

Identifies the drill move and classifies technique quality (proper vs. improper form).

STAGE 04 • REGRESSION

Biomechanical Scoring

Predicts continuous metrics: limb velocity, joint angle accuracy, injury risk & success probability.

Datasets & Benchmarks

  • SoccerNet (match & action benchmarks)
  • Kinetics-400 (human action pretraining)
  • Custom Solo Drills (recorded multi-angle reps)

Ethics & Safety Guardrails

  • On-device skeletal processing to protect youth video privacy
  • Tested across diverse heights, ages & body types
  • Injury-first biomechanical feedback thresholds

Active Engineering Challenges

1) Outdoor lighting, shadows & varied phone tripod angles • 2) Real-time mobile latency without thermal throttling • 3) Delivering concise, hands-free audio cues mid-drill.
